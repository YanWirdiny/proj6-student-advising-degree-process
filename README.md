# student-advising-degree-process

# Background

Academic advisors regularly help students determine whether they are making appropriate progress
toward graduation.
This requires comparing completed courses with degree requirements, prerequisites, credit requirements,
and other program rules.
Currently, much of this analysis is done manually.
Initial Project Goal
Develop a system that helps advisors evaluate a student’s academic progress toward completion of a
degree program.

# What You Know Initially

Students complete courses over multiple semesters.
Degree programs may contain:
• required courses;
• elective requirements;
• prerequisite relationships;
• minimum credit requirements; and
• categories of requirements.

The system should help an advisor determine what a student has completed and what remains.

# Your Team’s Responsibility
Your team must determine:
• how students and courses should be represented;
• how degree requirements should be represented;
• how prerequisite relationships should work;
• what counts as satisfying a requirement;
• how repeated or incomplete courses should be treated;
• what information should be shown to an advisor; and
• what rules apply when program requirements change.

Do not assume that degree requirements are simply a list of courses.
